**Jay Paul Company** closed a **$227.5 million** **C-PACE** loan with **Nuveen Green Capital** to refinance the construction loan on **Springdale Green**, its **881,427-square-foot** Class A office campus at 1011 Springdale Road in East Austin, The Real Deal reported. It is the largest C-PACE transaction on record in Texas history.

## Context

C-PACE (Commercial Property Assessed Clean Energy) financing lets property owners cover the cost of energy and water efficiency improvements through an assessment paid alongside property taxes, typically at rates more favorable than conventional financing. In the case of Springdale Green, the loan covers efficiency improvements already completed on the campus, which finished construction less than two years ago and is currently in the leasing phase. It is Jay Paul's second large transaction with Nuveen Green Capital, after the earlier financing of 200 Park, an office tower in San Jose, California. The size of the deal — the largest of its kind ever closed in Texas — comes at a time when much of the coverage of Austin offices has focused on oversupply and valuation adjustments downtown, making this transaction a signal that lenders are still willing to make large bets on quality office assets outside the central business district.

## What to do with this

- If you represent owners of Class A offices outside downtown Austin, use this deal as evidence that C-PACE is an active, large-scale route to refinance construction debt in today's market, not just a mechanism for small or boutique projects.
- For brokers and investors tracking the East Austin submarket, the fact that Springdale Green is drawing this level of capital while in lease-up (not yet stabilized) suggests lenders distinguish quality assets outside the core from the broader office oversupply narrative.
- If you advise developers evaluating energy-efficiency upgrades in existing buildings, this transaction is a useful benchmark for the current appetite of Nuveen Green Capital and other C-PACE lenders for large deals in Texas.
