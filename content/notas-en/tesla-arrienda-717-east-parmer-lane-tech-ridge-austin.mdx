**Tesla** signed an **82,000-square-foot** office lease at **717 East Parmer Lane**, in the **Tech Ridge** corridor of north Austin, The Real Deal reported. The space was part of the IT innovation center of **General Motors**, which retains another space on McCallen Pass.

## Context

Tech Ridge is a corporate corridor in north Austin. GM, which had its IT innovation center at 717 East Parmer Lane, retains another space on McCallen Pass, so its departure from that specific building does not mean GM is leaving Austin. Tesla has not specified what it will use the new space for, though it comes as the company has stepped up its focus on artificial intelligence and robotics (Optimus, Cybercab) from its Giga Texas base southeast of the city. The move adds to a string of Tesla corporate expansions in the Austin area during 2026, including a large industrial lease reported in July.
