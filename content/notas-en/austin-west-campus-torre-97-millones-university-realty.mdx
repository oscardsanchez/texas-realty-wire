University Realty, an Austin-based development firm, filed a $97 million construction permit with the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation to build a 30-story, 300-unit tower at 2307 Rio Grande Street in the West Campus neighborhood, one block from the University of Texas at Austin. According to the state filing, construction would begin in February and the project would total about 466,000 square feet.

## Context

West Campus concentrates much of Austin's high-rise student housing boom of recent years, driven by sustained demand from UT Austin and increasingly scarce, expensive land near campus. The new tower would sit just one block from Waterloo Tower, another 30-story student housing skyscraper in the same area, confirming that developers keep betting on building taller on the few infill lots still available in the neighborhood rather than looking for cheaper, more distant land.

## What to do with this

- If you work with student housing investors near UT Austin, this tower is one more sign that the West Campus submarket keeps absorbing capital for high-rises despite rising construction costs — it is worth monitoring how rent per unit moves in comparable buildings (Waterloo Tower, Dobie Center) once the project advances.
- For buyers or owners of infill lots in West Campus, the permit reaffirms that land values in the area remain supported by developers' appetite to densify vertically — a useful argument when appraising small parcels near campus.
- It is worth following the site plan review at the City of Austin, which according to local reports is still in process — the construction start schedule could shift before February.
