The City of Round Rock approved the development of a 71.88-acre tract that will allow KB Home to add approximately 330 new homes to its Salerno subdivision, just as the original 1,200-home development nears the end of construction. The expansion will be built in two phases: 181 single-family homes in the first and 149 in the second, according to city documents.

## Context

The tract now being developed was annexed into Round Rock in 2023, according to the city's planning director, and remained a growth reserve until this approval. That KB Home chose to expand Salerno rather than open a new subdivision elsewhere in the city confirms a pattern repeating across the Austin metro: large builders prefer to add phases to established communities with proven infrastructure and sales absorption, rather than take on the risk of a greenfield site in a new-home market that is still recovering.

## What to do with this

- Agents who already work listings in Salerno should anticipate that new-home inventory in the area will stay active for several more phases, which can put downward pressure on resale prices in the older phases if KB Home offers aggressive financing incentives on the new units.
- For buyers looking in northeast Round Rock, this expansion is a chance to enter a subdivision with established amenities and schools, rather than betting on an entirely new community with no absorption track record.
- It is worth following the Phase 1 building permit schedule, since it will mark when the first 181 units start reaching the market and will allow comparing list prices against earlier Salerno phases.
